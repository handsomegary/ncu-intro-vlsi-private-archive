HW4 Instructor Clarifications - English Translation

Translated from the instructor attachment supplied in the local course archive.
Dated updates are preserved, including the later change in base credit.

December 17
Area is length times width.

December 22
Required submission:
1. PDF containing layout, DRC, LVS, and pre/post-simulation waveform screenshots.
2. SPICE file after PEX.
3. Excel table with area in um^2 and energy in p-units, as labeled in the source.

Grading rules:
1. Complete deliverables with no deductions receive 10 base points.
2. Missing layout, DRC, LVS, pre/post-simulation waveform screenshots, written
   explanation, or calculations results in zero points.
3. Logic 1 must be within 1.6-1.8 V; logic 0 within 0-0.2 V. Each violating
   signal loses one point, up to five points. Glitches do not incur deductions
   if the output eventually reaches a valid logic level.
4. Measure layout length and width with a ruler and include a screenshot.
5. Only area and energy are ranked, not delay.
6. Deductions are applied after ranking.

December 23
Architecture and dimensions may change, but update the SPICE file accordingly.
Include pre/post-simulation waveform screenshots.

December 25
The area rectangle must cover ALL used materials, including NW, NIMP, POLY,
and metal (spelled MATAL in the source).
HW4 base credit is 15 points; the remaining 5 points come from ranking.
